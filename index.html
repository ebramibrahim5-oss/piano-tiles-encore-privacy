Piano Tiles: Encore

Privacy policy

Last updated September 20, 2026

This policy covers Piano Tiles: Encore and this information page. The current beta has revenue-generating advertising and store purchases disabled. Test builds may show clearly marked Google demo ads between games; these do not earn revenue, but the advertising SDK still processes information as described below. Paid access and server reward verification remain unavailable in this beta. This page will be updated before live activation.

Who to contact

Piano Tiles: Encore is a piano rhythm game for iOS and Android. For privacy questions or help with your information, contact ebramibrahim5@gmail.com. A parent or guardian may contact us on a child's behalf. Please do not send passwords, session tokens, payment-card numbers, identity documents, or unnecessary personal information.

Information on your device

The game stores settings, timing calibration, favorites, setlists, progress, scores, and downloaded music and charts on your device. You may choose a player display name; it is optional and is stored locally. You do not need to provide your real name to play. Device and audio-output details help select a local calibration profile. Calibration does not record your microphone.

When online commerce is used, the app also retains its private authentication record and a record of interrupted reward requests on your device, so it can authenticate requests and check whether a reward was completed.

The current game does not provide public chat, automatically publish your scores, or automatically post to social networks. If you choose to export or share a performance card, you control that action. The private music-upload tools are for the app operator, not a public player-upload service.

Online game services

When a build connects to online services, the service receives the information needed to answer the request, which can include your IP address, request timing, and basic app or network information. The app uses a randomly generated player identifier and a private session token to authenticate its commerce requests. These identify a game session or player record, rather than a public social profile.

The current native game keeps ordinary progress on the device and does not automatically upload local scores through the server's optional progress-storage interface. There is no customer cloud-account recovery or cross-device account-linking feature in the current beta.

Advertising and your choices

Google AdMob is the configured advertising provider. In builds where advertising is enabled, an interstitial ad may appear between completed games. A verified seven-day or lifetime no-ads purchase removes these forced result ads while that access is active. Rewarded ads remain optional. An eligible completed and verified rewarded ad provides one game without a result ad; ordinary play and replay do not require a rewarded ad.

When the advertising SDK runs, Google may process information such as IP address and approximate location derived from it, app or device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Which data is used depends on the platform, SDK settings, and available permissions. Consent messages and privacy controls are provided where required for the enabled advertising setup.

The app's prepared ad settings request child-directed and under-age treatment, contextual or non-personalized ads, and G-rated ad content. Publisher personalization and publisher first-party identifiers are disabled. The app does not request Apple's tracking permission. These settings do not mean that Google receives no information.

Reward verification sends a pseudonymous player and reward-request reference to the ad provider and receives a signed completion notice at the game's verification service. The service uses that notice to prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards. Verification records can include game, reward, and session identifiers, the song, difficulty and chart revision, and verification timestamps. Google's use of information is described in its Privacy Policy and its explanation of information used by partner sites and apps.

Purchases and no-ads access

The planned paid options are seven days without forced ads, with no automatic renewal, and lifetime removal of forced ads. Only products shown as available by your app store can be purchased. Your store presents the actual price and payment confirmation. Optional rewarded ads remain a separate choice.

Apple or Google handles payment through its own checkout. The game does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account number. To verify purchased access, the app sends the relevant store receipt or purchase token, product or transaction information, and its pseudonymous player identifier to the verification service. That service records information needed to associate and verify access, such as transaction-reference hashes, provider, product, validity period, and revocation or refund status.

Restore purchases checks existing access; it does not restart a seven-day period. The current guest identity does not provide verified recovery across reinstalls or new devices. Contact support about missing access without emailing a private session token or an unredacted receipt.

Children and families

The game is intended for a broad age range, including children. It does not ask players for their school, contacts, precise location, or microphone recordings. A parent or guardian should handle purchases and contact support for a child's privacy request. Any future change to the information requested or the advertising treatment will be reflected in this notice before that change is enabled.

Support messages

If you email us, we and our email service receive your email address, message, and attachments so that we can respond. You can describe a problem using your app version, device model, operating-system version, song, and the time the issue occurred. Remove unrelated personal information from screenshots and other attachments.

Storage and deletion

Local information remains on your device until removed through the device or app mechanisms available to you, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior. The current beta does not include an in-app erase-all or cloud-account deletion button. Contact us for help identifying the information involved in a request.

When online verification is enabled, server records are used to maintain paid access, verify rewards, and investigate support or fraud issues. The server implements authenticated deletion of a player's live record and related commerce records, but the current native app does not expose that endpoint as a customer deletion control. Ask support for assistance. Deleting game information does not submit a refund request to Apple or Google.

Live hosting, backup retention, and deletion procedures for the online services are not yet operating in the current beta. We will update this notice with the deployed service's arrangements before those features are activated. An offer or reward expiry time describes its validity and is not a promise that all related records are deleted at that moment.

Service providers and security

We use information for game operation, purchase and reward verification, support, and security. Apple, Google, Unity, and service providers that operate payment, advertising, email, or hosting services process information for their respective roles and under their own policies. Our prepared online connections use HTTPS, private operator controls require authentication, and downloaded game content is checked for integrity. No system can guarantee absolute security.

The game is built with Unity and includes its in-app purchasing SDK. When applicable SDK services are enabled, Unity may process device and app information, purchase-related events, and diagnostic information to operate and maintain those services. Its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy explains Unity's own processing and privacy choices.

This privacy page includes no advertising, analytics scripts, or embedded third-party content supplied by us. This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes connection information needed to deliver and secure the page, as described in its privacy statement. The game service's final hosting location and any additional transfer arrangements will be reflected here before live online features are activated.

Updates and requests

Contact ebramibrahim5@gmail.com to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy choices that apply to your information.
